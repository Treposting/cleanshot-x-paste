Features

Everything you capture, one shortcut away

Screenshots, recordings and everything you copy, handled from the menu bar.

Screenshots

Capture an area, a window or the whole screen, with an optional self-timer.

Screen recording

Record to MP4 at 30 or 60 fps, with computer audio if you want it. Export a GIF in one click.

Quick Access overlay

A thumbnail appears after each capture. Copy it, save it, or drag it straight into Slack or Mail.

Annotate

Add arrows, shapes, text, highlights and numbered steps. Pixelate anything private.

Copy text from anything

Select part of the screen and the text in it lands on your clipboard. OCR runs on your Mac.

Pin to screen

Keep a screenshot floating above your windows while you work. Scroll on it to fade it.

Clipboard history

Text, links, images and files are saved as you copy them. Search the history and paste any item back with one key.

Private

Nothing is uploaded, and passwords copied from password managers are never saved.

Two tools, one app

Capture tool and clipboard manager in one

You don't need separate apps for screenshots and clipboard history. ShotPaste does both.

Capture

Screenshots and recordings, ready to share.

  • Native crosshair selection. Press Space for window mode.
  • Annotation editor with undo, copy and save
  • MP4 recording with a menu-bar timer
  • Drag captures straight into any app

Paste

Everything you've copied, in a panel along the bottom of the screen.

  • Type to search, then filter by text, links, images or files
  • ⌘1–⌘9 pastes one of the first nine items instantly
  • Pin the items you reuse
  • Pastes straight into the app you were using
Every screenshot you take also lands in your clipboard history.

Install

Install in about a minute

Download

Get the latest .dmg from GitHub Releases.

Drag to Applications

Open the DMG and drag ShotPaste into Applications. On first launch, right-click the app and choose Open.

Allow access

Allow Screen Recording when macOS asks. Accessibility is optional and lets ShotPaste paste into other apps for you.

# or build it yourself (Xcode 15+)
git clone https://github.com/Treposting/cleanshot-x-paste.git
cd cleanshot-x-paste
make run      # or: make dmg
FAQ

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. ShotPaste is MIT-licensed open source. There's no account, subscription or telemetry.

Why does it need Screen Recording permission?

macOS requires it for any app that captures other windows, both for screenshots and for video. Captures stay on your Mac unless you share them yourself.

Does clipboard history store my passwords?

No. Password managers mark the passwords they copy as private, and ShotPaste skips anything marked that way. You can also pause history from the menu bar at any time.

Why does macOS say the app can't be opened?

Releases aren't notarized by Apple yet. Right-click the app and choose Open once, or run xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ShotPaste.app.
